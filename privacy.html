Privacy Policy

Jarvis Dashboard · Effective 16 November 2026

Who uses this app

Jarvis Dashboard is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

With the account holder's consent, it reads Gmail messages and Google Calendar events using read-only permissions (gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly). It cannot send, delete, label or change email, and it cannot change calendar events.

How the data is used

Only to show the owner what needs attention: replies to job applications, interview requests, bills, deadlines and similar items, and to summarize them privately for the owner.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored on the owner's private servers, readable only by the owner. Short summaries the app writes may be kept there too. To write a summary, the app may send message text to an AI model provider (mainly Anthropic's Claude), which processes it only to return that summary to the owner. Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it stops all reading immediately.

Contact

Questions can go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
